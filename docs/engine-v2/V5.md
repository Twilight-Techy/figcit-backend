# Rules Engine v5.0 in engine v2

v5.0 (November 2026, founder decisions of 11 Nov) replaces v3.3. It goes into the same rule store
as v3.3 did. The rules stay data that staff can edit and publish; nothing is trained, and no AI is
involved. This note says how v5.0 is read, what runs, and what is still to do.

## How it is read

- **A second workbook edition.** v5.0's tabs are new tables in the store (`capacity`, `feelings`,
  `body_senses`, `places`, `devices`, `colours`, `colour_rules`, `help_menu`, `pathways`,
  `plans_tools`, `time_demand`, `card_patterns`, `card_rules`, `gates`, `sequence`, `screens`,
  `accounts`, `actions`, `labels`, `outcomes`, `place_routes`, `stories`, `test_cases`,
  `change_log`). An upload is v5.0 when it has any v5.0 file, so neither edition reports the
  other's tabs as missing. The CSVs are in `docs/specs/rules-engine-v5/`, one per tab, exported
  from the workbook the way v3.3's were.
- **Examples, not rules.** Tab 18 (every colour outcome, 2,550 rows), Tab 19 (routes by place),
  Tab 20 (stories) and Tab 21 (test cases) are examples. They never run; the checks run against
  them.
- **The catalogue** (the standard values) comes from v5.0's own tabs: the 16 states, the bands and
  their colours, the 20 emotions and Something else (`family`, grouped Charged, Low, Shutdown,
  Settled, Unsorted), the slider levels (`intensity_band`: Mild 1-3, Moderate 4-6, Strong 7-8,
  Very strong 9-10), body signs and their groups, urgent signs, senses (`sensitivity_flag`) and the
  card patterns.
- **Rule set format 4** adds the values and facts v5.0 needs (below) and the `mode.no_words`
  effect. Format 3 rule sets (v3.3) run unchanged.

## What runs now: the card's words

The words are in the copy table (`x-copy.csv`), seeded from the workbook for a v5.0 upload
(`format/copy-v5.ts`), so staff can change any sentence without a release. `cardWordsV5` in
`engine/words-v5.ts` puts them together; the engine's card gives what it needs (`pattern`,
`feeling`, the deciding emotion, and `lowered`, set when the colour went below the state's own).

- **Headline:** Tab 11's, by pattern (`card.p<n>.headline`). The low group says "Let's start
  gently." for 4 and 48; Clear-headed or Empowered get 55's headline for 2 and 54.
- **Reason (RT-4):** "You're in {state}, feeling {emotion} ({strength}) and {other} with {two body
  signs at most}." Then, keeping three sentences at most: why the colour went down ("Feeling this
  strongly…", or "You seem switched off…" in no-words mode), an overload sense, where you are
  (discreet; open to audio with headphones; silent in an exam), the headline. A body need (hungry
  or thirsty, too hot or too cold) comes last, except in no-words mode. The urgent screen says
  only "You told us this is sudden or new. Please get urgent help now."
- **Task line (TK-2, TK-3):** "We've put {the first two kinds of task, in plain words} first
  today." Amber adds "'{top task}' is cut to one small step." Red and no-words mode say "Tasks
  kept, not suggested now.", and Red names each task that can't wait with its two offers.

## What runs now: plans (Tab 9)

- **Each tool's plan** is its Tab 17 row's rule (`mapping/v5-plans.ts`): a Prime or Premium tool is
  of that tier and of `paid`. Moving a tool between plans is an edit of that rule (ENT-9). Create
  is free: Tab 17's newer row says "Free (limited) / Prime".
- **The plan** is `signals.plan`: `free`, `prime`, `premium`, `organisation_seat` or `pilot`. No
  plan reads as Free. Premium, an organisation seat and the pilot include everything.
- **ENT-1:** the primary is always something the plan opens. Where the best fit is paid, its free
  version leads (`tools.fallback`, new in format 4) and the paid tool is offered beside it,
  labelled (T-20). The free versions are read from the labels, for the founder to confirm (for
  example What helped → Make sense of today, Notes for my appointment → Write it out).
- **ENT-3 and ENT-11:** under strain (Amber, Red, a difficult feeling at Strong or above, a recent
  safety event, during a session) no tool the plan lacks; otherwise at most one.
- **Labels:** a paid tool on the card says which plan it needs (`plan: 'prime' | 'premium'`), so
  the app can show the lock (ENT-12).
- **Safety floor:** Tab 9's floor column, as tool codes, is the `floor` tier. The app's pathway
  screens use it in Amber and Red (T-19).

## What runs now: the Help menu, the gates and the safety rows

- **The menu (Tab 7):** every card says the Help menu for its light (`menu.cards`, the eight
  support cards in order, and `menu.more`, the 'More needs' list). Each card's 'Shown in' is a rule
  that hides it elsewhere: no Reflect in Amber or Red or with a feeling at Strong or above (RF-1);
  Meditate only in Green, Yellow and Blue, in Amber only if calm, and not under MD-2. Red puts
  Help me calm down, Too much in my head and I want to talk to someone first. ME-4: Losing someone
  puts Reflect, Connect and Meditate first (the only order Tab 15 gives).
- **A card picked** (`help.card`) sets the pathway (SEQ-2) and, in Green, Yellow or Blue, its
  pattern's headline (Help me calm down 48, Too much in my head 47, Too much around me 13, find
  the words 9, make sense 32, my practice 38, talk to someone 11; get something done is the task,
  or 46 with none). With a task in Green, Yellow or Blue it asks "Which first?" (pattern 43, SEQ-1);
  in Amber or Red the support comes first and the task waits (5, 7). A blocked card leads to the
  safe route and the card says why in one line (`helpSwap`, wording `help.swap.<card>`): Reflect
  blocked is pattern 40 (Empty your head), Meditate blocked is 41 (Ground) or, in Amber with
  distress, Regulate with the own practice beside it. The 'More needs' each open their route.
  The user's own practice opens in any light (ENT-4). I need help now is Help/SOS (pattern 28).
- **Gates (Tab 12):** RF-1 (no Reflect in Amber or Red), RF-2 (not after a worse re-check), MD-1
  and MD-2 (guided Meditate never leads in Red, in Amber with distress, or in no-words mode, with

- **Quick check-in (P42, CTX-U):** `checkin.mode: 'quick'` gives the discreet tools and the link

- **Safety:** SF-1 (`safety.risk`, the API's text check) and SF-2 (Red 7+ days, 14 without

## What runs now: the check-in options

- **The options** (`x-input-mappings.csv`) are seeded from the same rows as the catalogue
  (`format/input-seed-v5.ts`): emotions, body signs, urgent signs, senses, places, devices, task
  chips and the Help menu's cards (new questions `body`, `urgent` and `help`). Each option's Group
  is the plan that can pick it: `free`, `prime` (Tab 9: Prime and above) or `always_free` (Anxious,
  Overwhelmed, Teary or sad, Not good enough, Shut down, Something else, the urgent signs and
  I need help now; ENT-13).
- **`factsFromCheckInV5`** turns the app's check-in into engine input. It drops an option the
  plan can't pick (ENT-12), anything past two emotions or, on Free, three tasks, and a Quick
  check-in's body, senses and place (CTX-U), and says why for each. Urgent signs are always read.
  The API adds the plan, Life right now, the safety check and the other signals.

**Checked:** `test/v5-check-in.test.ts`.

## What runs now: the API

- **`POST /v1/decisions/v5/preview`** runs a v5.0 check-in as the person goes and stores
  nothing: the colour, whether it went down, no-words mode, the plain sentence (RT-4) and the Help
  menu with each card's words (Tab 13 steps 3 to 7).
- **`POST /v1/decisions/v5`** records the decision like every engine v2 decision (so it replays the
  same) and returns the card as the app shows it: headline, reason, task line, each button with
  Tab 17's words or the action's words and the plan a paid tool needs, the suggestion, prompts,
  the task titles in the colour's order, and the one-line swap reason.
- The server adds the plan in v5.0's names (Free, Prime, Premium; an organisation seat is
  Premium), Life right now from Me (ME-4), the account's signals and re-check, and the safety check
  on the free text (Something else, task titles; SF-1). Task titles never reach the engine.
- The route answers 409 `ruleset_not_v5` while v3.3 is the published version.
- **Publishing:** a version needs options for the questions its own edition asks
  (`questionsOf`). The publish sweep runs v5.0's tasks and plans, and its fixed rules read v5.0's
  plans; "no task first in Amber or Red" lets Amber's one small step through (TK-3; Tab 18 row
  951), as v5.0 says, and still holds the task itself and anything in Red.

**Checked:** `apps/api/test/decisions-v5.e2e.ts` imports and publishes the v5.0 workbook, runs
the preview and the decision, and publishes v3.3 again. `test/v5-publish.test.ts`: v5.0 breaks no
fixed rule on the sweep.
