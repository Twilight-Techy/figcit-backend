**SERVICE 1 · THE FIGCIT APP**

**Support that fits how you feel right now**

Check in with your energy and mood, and Figcit suggests what will help within the hour: a short reset, a way to sort a busy head, help finding the words, or the smallest next step.

## How it works

1.  **Check in.** Tell us your perceived energy and mood, how you feel at the moment, if you want to.

2.  **See your light.** A gentle traffic light shows your capacity right now.

3.  **Choose what fits.** Regulate, offload, express, connect, act, reflect or adapt. “Not now” is always an answer.

## What’s inside

> **Regulate**  
> Short calming resets with sound and breathing, to settle your body and mind.
>
> **Brain Dump**  
> Empty a busy head and find one first step you can manage.
>
> **Find the words**  
> Help to ask for what you need at work, at college or at home.

> **Gentle by design**  
> No streaks, no guilt, and reminders that respect your energy.

> **Low-stimulation mode**

> **Safe routes**

**SERVICE 2 · CONSULTING AND TRAINING**

**Adjust before absence, burnout or dropout**

We help employers, colleges and universities see where their demands, spaces and routines don’t fit people and change them, so people stay well, stay in and do their best work.

## CAAM audit: from £3,000/ organisation 

Our Capacity Adaptive Adjustment Model audit takes four to six weeks. We listen through an anonymous questionnaire, interviews and focus groups, look at your policies and spaces, and give you a clear report with prioritised adjustments, an action plan linked to your Equality Act 2010 duties, and a CAAM score you can track over time.

## FITS training: from £1,200 per group

FITS stands for Functioning, Inclusion, Trauma-informed and Sensory-friendly practice. **Awareness** is a half-day session for all staff. **Practitioner** is a two-day certified course for managers, HR, tutors and support teams. Licensed **CAAM Consultant** training follows.

## App seats and monitoring

Figcit app seats support your people every day at £6/seat monthly, and quarterly monitoring (from £400 a month) to show what has changed.

## How we work with you

A free discovery call → a scoped proposal → your CAAM audit → report and FITS training → an app pilot → ongoing monitoring.

**OUR STORY**

> *“One afternoon at work, a panic attack turned into a full nervous system shutdown. For three hours I couldn’t move, speak clearly or get myself help. The help I needed only arrived after the crash. And that crash led to two weeks or absence in school and work”*

That afternoon gave me a clear question: what if support started with the capacity a person has right now and the places around them adapted to it? Our 2026 research with CESNE gave that question weight: 73% of 244 people had left a job, course or opportunity because their needs weren’t met. They didn’t need fixing. The fit did.

**OUR APPROACH**

Before any task, we ask four questions. **What matters?** **What kind of task is it?** **What can I manage right now?** **What kind of space am I in?** Our Capacity Matrix shows energy and mood as a gentle traffic light, so the task, the timing and the space can be matched to the person. Its sixteen-part design draws on the Yoruba Ifá system as cultural inspiration; our evidence comes from research and evaluation.

# Part B · Build Notes for the Designer and Developer

## Navigation

Main menu: **Services** (The Figcit app · Consulting and training · The Figcit Hub · Research, evaluation and policy) · **Who we help** (For you · For employers · For colleges and universities · For partners) · **About** (Who we are · Our team) · **Contact**. Header buttons: “Get early access” (primary) and “Book a conversation” (secondary). A “Comfort” button sits in every header.

## Look and feel

- Colours: Fig Purple \#5B3358 (primary), Citrus Orange \#E08830 (highlights and large text only; \#9A5712 for small orange text), Rind Teal \#3E7A72, Terracotta Bloom \#A85D63, Fig Ink \#2A1E2E (body text), Pith Cream \#F5EEDE (section backgrounds).

- Type: Figtree for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for body text (18px desktop, 17px mobile, line height 1.6). Self-host both fonts.

- Generous white space, one idea per section, short paragraphs and clear buttons — a landing page, not a report.

- Photography of real, diverse people resting, learning and working. No puzzle pieces, lightning-bolt brains or head-clutching images.

- Traffic lights always carry a text label: Available, Conditional, Constrained, Very constrained, Recovery.

## Comfort and accessibility

- Comfort panel on every page: low-stimulation mode (muted colours, no motion, extra spacing), three text sizes, and light or dark theme. Respect reduced-motion settings. No autoplay, carousels or pop-ups.

- WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard and screen-reader friendly, visible focus, alt text, 4.5:1 text contrast, works at 320px wide and at 200% zoom.

## Forms

- **Get early access:** first name, email, “I am a…” (individual, student, employer, other), confirm 16 or over, consent.

- **Book a conversation:** name, email, organisation, role, sector, what you’d like to discuss, any adjustments that would help, consent.

- **Hub interest:** first name, email, town or city, how you might use the Hub, consent.

- **Partner with us:** name, email, organisation, type of partnership, message, consent.

- Never ask for a diagnosis, health details or date of birth. Store data in the UK or EU. Privacy-friendly spam protection, no puzzles.

## Technical

- Editable CMS, UK or EU hosting, HTTPS, security headers and backups.

- No non-essential cookies before consent; privacy-friendly analytics.

- Meet Google’s “good” Core Web Vitals (LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1).

- One H1 per page, SEO titles and descriptions as given on each page, Organization structured data and an XML sitemap.

- Printed QR codes point to https://www.figcit.co.uk — keep it live.

## Launch phases

- **By December 2026:** Home, Services, the four Who we help pages, About, Team, Contact, Questions, legal pages and all forms.

- **January 2027:** app store badges, live pricing, first news posts, brochure download.

- **January 2028:** Hub booking details for the pilot.
